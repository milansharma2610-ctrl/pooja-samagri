Significance of Rudrabhishek Puja

Rudrabhishek is an ancient Vedic ritual dedicated to Lord Shiva, invoking his benevolent Rudra form. The abhishek is performed by chanting the sacred Sri Rudram from the Krishna Yajurveda, accompanied by continuous offerings of Panchamrit (milk, curd, honey, pure ghee, cane sugar), Gangajal, and bilva leaves over the Shiva Lingam.

“Just as watering the roots nourishes the entire tree, performing abhishekam to the Shiva Lingam pleases all devatas and brings mental tranquility, health, and family prosperity.” — Shiva Purana
